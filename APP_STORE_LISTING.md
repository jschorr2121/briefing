## Description
**Your news, your topics, your schedule.**

Briefing is a personalized news app that generates AI-powered briefings on the topics YOU choose. No algorithmic feeds. No one-size-fits-all newsletters. Just the news that matters to you.

**How it works:**
1. Pick your topics — AI & Tech, Finance, Sports, Crypto, Startups, or anything custom
2. Generate a briefing — AI searches the web and creates a polished summary with sources
3. Listen, read, or email — 12 voice options, speed control, markdown export, or send to your inbox

**Features:**
• Custom topics — add any topic you care about
• Real-time web search — always fresh, never recycled content
• Audio briefings — 12 natural AI voices with speed control
• Scheduled delivery — daily briefing emails on autopilot
• Source transparency — every story links to the original article
• Dark mode — easy on the eyes, day and night
• Export — download as markdown or email to yourself

**Briefing Pro:**
• Unlimited daily briefings
• Priority generation
• All 12 premium voices
• Scheduled email delivery

**Free tier:**
• 3 briefings per day
• All topics and features
• Audio generation
