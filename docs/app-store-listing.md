## Description (4000 chars max)

**Briefing** turns your morning scroll into a focused 5-minute ritual. Choose your topics, and our AI delivers concise, accurate news briefings powered by real-time web search.

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Pick your topics — AI & Tech, Finance, World News, Sports, or anything you care about
2. Generate your briefing — AI searches the web and synthesizes the latest developments
3. Read or listen — 12 premium voices to fit your commute, workout, or morning coffee
4. Schedule daily emails — wake up to personalized news in your inbox

**KEY FEATURES**

• **Real-Time Search** — Not yesterday's news. Briefing searches the web at generation time for the freshest stories.

• **Listen Anywhere** — High-quality text-to-speech with 12 voices. Perfect for your commute, gym, or multitasking.

• **Daily Emails** — Schedule briefings to arrive in your inbox at your preferred time. No app required.

• **Topic Customization** — Mix and match topics. Go broad with "World News" or niche with "Electric Vehicles" or "Broadway."

• **Export & Share** — Save briefings as markdown or share with friends.

• **Dark Mode** — Easy on the eyes, day or night.

**WHO IT'S FOR**

- Busy professionals who want news without the noise
- Students catching up between classes
- Anyone tired of doomscrolling but wanting to stay informed
- Podcast listeners who want a quick news hit

**PRIVACY FIRST**

Your topics and briefings stay private. We don't sell your data or track your reading habits beyond what's necessary to deliver the service.
