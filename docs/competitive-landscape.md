# Briefing — Competitive Landscape (Feb 2026)

## Briefing's Position
**Fully customizable, topic-based AI news briefings** with audio, email delivery, and scheduled automation. Users choose exactly what they care about — not what an algorithm thinks they want.

## Direct Competitors

### AI News Apps
| App | Model | Audio? | Custom Topics? | Scheduling? | Pricing |
|-----|-------|--------|---------------|-------------|---------|
| **Particle** | Multi-source AI summaries | Audio summaries | Feed-based, not topics | No | Free |
| **Concise** | AI-powered news briefing | Yes | Some personalization | No | Freemium |
| **Yahoo News (Artifact DNA)** | AI-curated feed + "Daily Digest" | Yes (6-8 min audio) | Algorithm-driven | Weekday afternoons only | Free |
| **Readless** | Newsletter consolidation | No | Based on existing subs | Scheduled digest | $9/mo |
| **Briefing ⭐** | **Topic-driven AI generation** | **Yes (voice picker)** | **Fully custom** | **Daily email scheduling** | **3 free + Pro** |

### AI Newsletters (Human-Written + AI-Assisted)
| Newsletter | Focus | Subscribers | Model |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-------|
| **The Rundown AI** | AI/tech news | 1M+ | Human-curated, daily |
| **TLDR** | Tech news digest | 1.25M+ | Human-curated, daily |
| **Morning Brew** | Business/finance | 4M+ | Human-written, daily |
| **The Hustle** | Business/tech | 2.5M+ | Human-written, daily |

### Key Differences
These newsletters are **one-size-fits-all** — everyone gets the same content. Briefing generates **unique content per user** based on their chosen topics.

## Briefing's Differentiators
1. **User-defined topics** — not algorithmic guessing, not human editors. You pick what matters.
2. **On-demand + scheduled** — generate anytime OR get daily email briefings automatically
3. **Audio with voice picker** — 12 OpenAI voices, speed control, download
4. **Real-time web search** — content is fresh, not recycled RSS
5. **Source transparency** — every story links to original articles
6. **Export options** — email, audio, markdown download

## What Others Do Better
- **Particle**: Multi-perspective coverage (same story from multiple sources side-by-side)
- **Yahoo News**: Massive scale, breaking news push notifications
- **Readless**: Newsletter consolidation (different problem — aggregates existing subs)
- **Morning Brew / TLDR**: Editorial voice, community, brand loyalty

## Opportunities
- **"Commute mode"** — Jake's original vision: voice-interactive briefing during commute
- **Podcast-style delivery** — two-voice conversational format (like NotebookLM)
- **Social/shared briefings** — share your briefing publicly, see what topics others follow
- **Briefing Hub** — already built; expand into community-curated topic templates
- **Push notifications** — breaking news alerts for tracked topics
- **Mobile app** — Capacitor scaffolding already exists, ship to App Store
- **Deeper personalization** — learn from reading patterns, surface related topics
- **Integration with Second Brain** — auto-save interesting articles to Notion/Obsidian
- **Slack/Discord bot** — deliver briefings into team channels
- **API access** — let developers build on top of Briefing's generation engine

## Threats
- OpenAI/Perplexity add scheduled "daily brief" features natively
- Apple Intelligence integrates personalized news summaries into iOS
- Google Gemini adds scheduled news digests to Gmail
- Newsletter platforms (Beehiiv, Substack) add AI personalization

## Market Gap
The market splits into two camps:
1. **Human-curated newsletters** — great editorial voice, zero personalization
2. **AI news feeds** — personalized but shallow, algorithm-driven, no scheduling

Briefing sits in the **unoccupied middle**: AI-generated depth with full user control over topics, plus multi-format delivery (web, email, audio). Nobody else lets you say "give me AI, startups, and Jets football" and get a polished briefing with audio every morning.

## Bottom Line
Briefing's wedge is **user agency** — you define what matters, AI does the work. The market is moving toward personalization but most apps still use opaque algorithms. Briefing's transparency (you pick topics, you see sources) is a trust advantage. Ship the mobile app and conversational audio to widen the moat.
